User guide

Find the steps for your workspace.

{% if coordinator %}

Set up an internship batch

  1. In Program setup, create an internship batch and enable its domains. Add teams in Team management if you want multiple teams per domain.
  2. Register interns in Intern Master or import the Intern Master template. Enter each intern’s college and domain. Excel intake assigns the team automatically and shows the allocation in the preview.
  3. Share each private account activation link with the verified intern. Designate a Team Lead by editing the team member's intern record.

Assign and verify work

  1. Assign a task with a start date, due date, and instructions, or upload the task register.
  2. Open Submission reviews and check the intern’s work details and uploaded document.
  3. Mark the task complete or request a revision with comments and a new deadline. The intern can see the decision in My reviews.
  4. Use Reports to export follow-ups and check team completion.
{% else %}

Submit an assignment

  1. Open My work and check the start date, due date, and task instructions.
  2. Enter your work details and upload the document. Use the submission manifest in Upload center for multiple tasks.
  3. Open My reviews for the coordinator’s completion status or revision comments. Check Inbox for team messages.
{% if team_lead %}

Monitor your team

Open Team workspace to view assignment status and deadlines. Choose a teammate to send an individual message to their inbox.

{% endif %} {% endif %}

Use a prebuilt Excel template

Fill the Data sheet and retain its headers. After upload, review the preview and download row errors if needed. Fix the source workbook, upload the corrected file, and confirm the validated batch.
